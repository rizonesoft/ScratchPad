# Owed case retirements

The retirement ledger for owed night cases (D00 T02 §52 item 8). An owed case carries its obligation across population changes: when a regen removes, renames, or reparameterizes it, the nightly holds it (no longer listed, or its identity token changed) instead of closing it on a replacement that shares its truncated display prefix. A held case leaves the owed set only through a row here.

A row names the owed case exactly as the morning report prints it, the evidence that its obligation is met or moot (a commit, a collection log, or the case that replaces it and its green run), who approved the retirement, and the date. A row without evidence or an approver is ignored and named in the report.
